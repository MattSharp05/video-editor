The lead at every moment, the move that makes the next frame, and the carrier of each hand-off.
No cuts. (Principle 1, `../PRINCIPLES.md`.)

| s | Lead | Move | Next lead | Carrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Techniques used: transform (point→card), under the hood (reading), split + travel (names→pins),
map becomes a floor, merge (route), exploded interface in reverse (assemble, fold flat), component
macro, icon performs its verb, callback.
