What happens with your picks

  1. Each pick becomes that moment's default sound in the next video, and the options you passed over are noted.
  2. Every option carries its measured character (brightness, length, ring, attack), so your picks also teach a profile, e.g. "dark, dry, soft attack": future candidates are ranked by how close they are to it.
  3. When a new sound comes in (a new library, a Freesound pull), it gets measured and the closest matches to your profile come first, so most rounds only need a yes or no.
