# Instagram caption: s5 ball stack

Drop a tennis ball on a basketball. Guess how high it goes 🎾🏀

The basketball squashes into the ground and springs back up while the tennis ball is still falling onto it, so the
tennis ball gets launched off a ball that's rushing up underneath it.
With perfectly bouncy balls (and a much heavier bottom ball) it could leave almost 3× as fast, and 3× the speed means
9× the height. Real balls lose some bounce, so expect about 3–4× the drop.

What did you guess? Try it outside and tell me how high yours went 👇
