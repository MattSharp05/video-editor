# Instagram caption: s1 Heart in the middle

Your heart isn't really on the left. 🫀

It sits behind your breastbone, near the middle of your chest. About two-thirds of it is left of centre, and it leans
so its tip points down to the left. That tip is where you can often feel the beat.
That's why CPR goes on the lower half of the breastbone, not the left side: push hard and fast, 100-120 times a
minute, until help comes.

Would you have pushed on the left? Save this for the day it matters.
