# Instagram caption: s1 Knuckle crack

Cracking your knuckles doesn't give you arthritis. 🦴

That pop isn't bone. Pulling the joint apart drops the pressure in the fluid inside it, and a gas bubble forms right at
the pop. A knuckle usually can't crack again for about 20 minutes.
One doctor cracked only his left hand for 50 years (36,500+ cracks). Neither hand got arthritis, and a study of 215
people found no link either.

Are you a knuckle cracker? If it ever hurts or swells, stop and get it checked.
