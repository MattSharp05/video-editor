# Caption: s2 coin rotation

How many times does the coin turn? The SAT got this one wrong 🪙

Roll a coin all the way around an identical coin and it turns twice, not once: one turn from rolling along the edge,
one more from going around the circle.

In May 1982 the SAT asked it with a circle a third as wide. The answer key said 3. The real answer is 4, and it wasn't
one of the choices. Three students caught it, and the College Board recalculated about 300,000 students' math scores.
(Counting turns as seen from the table. Seen from the big circle it's 3, which is why the test makers called the item
ambiguous.)

Try it with two coins. Did you guess two? 👇
