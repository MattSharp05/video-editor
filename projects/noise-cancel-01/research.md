# Noise cancel 01: research (fresh research agent, 2026-11-07)
Opened: Wikipedia "Noise-cancelling headphones", "Active noise control"; Lueg patent US 2,043,416 (Google Patents).
Snippets: Bose history, Sony WH-1000XM5 pages, AirPods Pro coverage, RTINGS, latency papers, Soundly audiologist.
- Sound = air pushed and pulled (pressure wave; crest = push, trough = pull).
- Feedforward mics (outside) + feedback mics (inside); hybrid = both.
- Chip makes an inverted copy ("anti-noise"); speaker plays it with your music; push meets pull -> mostly cancels at the
  ear (destructive interference), only in a small zone at the ear, partial. Music is not cancelled (the processor knows
  the music signal).
- Best on steady low sounds (engines, AC, rumble; best below ~1 kHz); poor on sudden/high sounds (voices, alarms);
  cushions/tips block highs passively. Best ANC >15 dB in bass (RTINGS).
- Sony WH-1000XM5: 8 mics; AirPods Pro adapt 200x/s (ANC). Lueg patent 1936; Bose pilot headsets 1980s.
- Myths: "just blocks sound" (it adds sound), "cancels everything", "anti-noise is silence", "bad for ears" (no known
  harm; pressure feeling is perception). Safe: "most of the hum cancels out right at your ear".
