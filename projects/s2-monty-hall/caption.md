# Caption: s2 Monty Hall

Nearly a thousand PhDs told her she was wrong. She wasn't. 🚪🐐🚗

Pick a door. The host, who knows where the car is, always opens a goat door you didn't pick and lets you switch.
Staying wins 1 time in 3. Switching wins 2 in 3.

Still not convinced? Picture 100 doors: you pick one, he opens 98 goats. Would you really keep yours?

Marilyn vos Savant answered this in her Parade column in September 1990, and about 10,000 letters came in, most saying
she was wrong (close to 1,000 signed by PhDs). The math sided with her.

Would you have switched? 👇
