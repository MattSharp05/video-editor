# Instagram caption: s4 ISS gravity (brain teaser)

Astronauts float on the Space Station. So how much gravity is up there? 🧑‍🚀 Guess before you watch the answer.

About 90% of what you feel on the ground. Build a tower 400 km tall and step off: you'd fall.
The crew floats because the whole station is falling too, while flying sideways at ~7.7 km/s, so the curved Earth
keeps dropping away beneath it. It never hits the ground. That's free fall, not zero gravity.

What did you guess: none, half, or almost all? 👇
