In 1910, a woman vanished in London. Then a secret message from a ship helped catch the man accused of her murder.
This is Caught by Wireless. Part one: the missing singer.
Cora Crippen was a singer with big hats and lots of friends.
Her husband was Hawley Crippen, a small, quiet doctor with round glasses and a big mustache.
One winter night, two friends came over for dinner and cards. When they left, Cora waved goodbye from the front steps.
Her friends never saw her again.
Soon, the doctor told them Cora had gone to America.
Then he said she got sick there, and died.
But her friends had questions. Where in America? When was the funeral? The doctor's answers were fuzzy.
And there was something else. The doctor's typist, Ethel, was wearing Cora's jewelry.
So Cora's friends went to the police at Scotland Yard.
Inspector Walter Dew came to ask the doctor some questions.
This time, the doctor told a new story. He said Cora had left him and run away.
He even showed the inspector around his house, all the way down to the cellar.
The inspector left. But when he came back, the doctor and Ethel were gone.
So the police searched the house again. Under the cellar floor, they found human remains.
And the doctor? He had vanished.
