# Instagram caption: s5 kettle roar

Why is your kettle LOUDEST right before it boils? 🫖🔊

The water touching the hot element boils first, into tiny bubbles of steam. But the water above is still cooler, so
each bubble rises, cools and collapses back into water. All those tiny collapses ring through the kettle: that's the
roar. Once all the water is near 100 °C the bubbles make it to the top and pop gently, so it goes quieter, right at
the boil, a moment before the switch clicks off.

Listen next time you put the kettle on: when the roar fades, it's boiling 👇
