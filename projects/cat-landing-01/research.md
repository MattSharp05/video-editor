# Cat landing 01: research (fresh research agent, 2026-11-07)
Opened: Wikipedia "Cat righting reflex"; Public Domain Review (Marey 1894); IFLScience; UNC Charlotte (Gbur);
Smithsonian; NASA NTRS citation (Kane & Scher). Snippets: Nature 51:80 (Marey), ScienceAlert (2026 study timing).
- Inner ear (vestibular) + eyes sense "up". No spin to start, nothing to push on: total angular momentum stays zero.
- Bend and twist (Kane & Scher 1969, Int. J. Solids Struct.): bends at the middle, front and back halves turn opposite ways.
- Tuck and turn (Marey 1894): front legs in (spins easily), back legs out (barely turns; ~90 vs ~10 deg), then swap.
  Real cats mix both (Gbur). 2026 study (Higurashi et al.): front half finishes first (72-94 ms ahead) - small sample.
- Tail helps a little; tail-less cats turn over fine (Gbur).
- Landing: spread legs (drag), bend legs to absorb. Reflex appears ~3-4 weeks, done ~6-9 weeks.
- Turning time: no primary source for minimum height; a 1 m fall lasts ~0.45 s -> "less than half a second" (can).
- Myths: always lands safely (Whitney & Mehlhaff 1987: ~90% had chest injuries; survivorship bias); higher is safer
  (not shown). Safety: never drop a cat; screens closed.
