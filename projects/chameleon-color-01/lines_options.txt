Most people think chameleons change color to hide. But mostly, they change color to talk.
A bright red chameleon is saying back off. A dark one might just be cold.
A chameleon's skin is full of tiny crystals. They work like tiny mirrors.
When the crystals sit close together, blue light bounces back. Blue and yellow make green.
When the crystals spread apart, red light bounces back instead.
Meet Ziggy. It is six in the morning, and Ziggy is cold.
So Ziggy turns dark. Dark colors soak up the warm sun.
At lunch, another chameleon shows up. Ziggy turns bright to say this is my branch.
