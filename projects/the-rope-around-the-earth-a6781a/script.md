## Draft caption
One extra meter of rope around the whole Earth lifts it about 16 cm off the ground, all the way round. A cat could
crawl under. The gap is 1 m ÷ 2π, and it's the same for a tennis ball. What did you guess?

## Claims to verify
1. Earth's equatorial circumference is about 40,075 km (WGS-84 equatorial radius 6,378.137 km x 2 pi = 40,075.0 km).
2. Circumference C = 2 pi r, so a ring 1 m longer has a radius 1/(2 pi) m = 0.159 m larger: about 16 cm, for any r.
3. A tennis ball: diameter 6.54-6.86 cm (ITF), radius about 3.3 cm; the result does not depend on it.
4. "A rope lifted one step gets about six steps longer": raising a circle's radius by d adds 2 pi d = 6.28 d to its
   length. "About a sixth of a meter": 1/6.28 = 0.159 m.
5. A cat can crawl under 16 cm: cats stand about 23-25 cm at the shoulder and squeeze under much lower gaps when
   crouched (framed as "enough to crawl under", shown crouching).
