# Caption: infinity hotel 01

Trying to conceptualize infinity? Check into a hotel that never ends 🏨♾️

A full hotel with endless rooms can still fit one more guest (everyone moves to the next room) and even an endless bus
(everyone doubles their room number, so every odd room is free).

But give a room to every number between 0 and 1 and it breaks: change room 1's first digit, room 2's second, and so on,
and you've built a number that isn't in any room. However you fill the hotel, some numbers are left out. Some
infinities really are bigger than others.

Which infinity do you think is bigger? 👇
