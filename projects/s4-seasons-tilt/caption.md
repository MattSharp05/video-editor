# Instagram caption: s4 Seasons tilt

Earth is closest to the Sun in January. So why is it winter? ☀️❄️

Summer isn't about distance: we're only ~3% closer in early January than in July.
It's the tilt. When your half of Earth leans toward the Sun, sunlight hits almost straight down and packs its heat
into a small patch. In winter the same beam comes in at a slant and spreads over about twice the ground.
(Longer summer days add to it, too.)
