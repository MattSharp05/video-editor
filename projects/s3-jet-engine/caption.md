A jet engine doesn't really push the plane with fire. 🔥➡️💨

In a modern airliner engine, only about 1 in 10 parts of the air goes through the core, where it's squeezed and burned. That hot blast spins the turbines, and the last turbine turns the giant fan.
The other 9 parts never burn: the fan just shoves them backward around the core, and that big, slower stream makes most of the thrust (Rolls-Royce says over 85% on its Trent 1000).

Did you know your plane is mostly pushed by a fan? 👇
