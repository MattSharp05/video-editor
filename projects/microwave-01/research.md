# Microwave 01: research (fresh research agent, 2026-11-07)
Opened: FDA "Microwave Ovens"; C. Baird (WTAMU) "Why are the microwaves in a microwave oven tuned to water?";
Pueschner penetration-depth table; Lemelson-MIT Percy Spencer; Wikipedia Percy Spencer. Snippets: APS News (403),
Chem 13 News chocolate test, Straight Dope (door mesh).
- Magnetron makes microwaves (FDA: "similar to radio waves"), 2.45 GHz, wavelength ~12.2 cm; metal box reflects;
  door mesh holes ~1-2 mm << 12 cm (light gets through, microwaves don't).
- Field swings 2.45 billion times/s ("swings back and forth", not "spins"); water is polar (+H side, -O side); molecules
  twist a little and bump neighbours -> heat (dielectric heating). NOT water resonance (Baird: first resonance >1 THz;
  2.45 GHz is an FCC allocation).
- Heats the outer layer (~1 cm in moist food: potato 0.8-0.9, meat 0.9-1.2, bread 2-5 cm; Pueschner); inside by
  conduction. FDA: "do not cook food from the 'inside out'". Say "outer layer", not a number.
- Standing waves -> hot/cold spots; turntable moves food through them. Chocolate test spots ~6 cm apart (half wavelength).
- Also heats fats/sugars/salty water ("mostly the water"); ice heats poorly. Not radioactive (FDA).
- Percy Spencer patent filed 1945; melted candy bar is "legend" (Reader's Digest 1958) -> "the story goes".
- Metal: sharp points/thin edges spark; walls are metal; FDA advises against metal.
