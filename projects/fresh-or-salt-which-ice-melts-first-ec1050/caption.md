# Caption (render checkpoint)

Two ice cubes, fresh water and salt water. Which melts first? 🧊 Pause and guess.
The fresh-water cube wins. Its cold meltwater is heavier, so it sinks and lets warmer water reach the cube. In salt water, the meltwater is lighter, so it floats around the cube as a cold layer.
Try it at home: two glasses of room-temperature water, a spoonful of salt in one, and ice cubes with a drop of food colouring frozen in.
