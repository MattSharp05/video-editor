# Caption (render checkpoint)

A bridge this long should snap. Here's why it doesn't. 🌉

The road doesn't stand on legs: it hangs from thin cables on two huge main cables. Steel pulled tight (tension) carries the weight up to the towers, the towers are pressed straight down (compression), and giant concrete anchors hold the cable ends back. Each Golden Gate main cable is made of 27,572 steel wires.
