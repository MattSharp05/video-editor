# Caption (Instagram, @storytime_scroll)

This shrimp punches so fast the water bursts. 🦐💥
The peacock mantis shrimp's club can hit up to 80 km/h. The water at the shell tears into vapour bubbles, and when they collapse they hit the shell a second time: one punch, two hits.
