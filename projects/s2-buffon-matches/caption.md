# Caption: s2 Buffon's matches

You can find π by dropping matches on the floor 🔥📏

Use matches exactly as long as your floorboards are wide. About 64% of them land across a crack: exactly 2 out of π
on average.

Why: bend a wire into a hoop as wide as a board. Wherever it lands, it crosses the cracks twice, and it's π matches
long. On average only length counts, so π matches make 2 crossings.

We dropped 20,000 (one random run, not cherry-picked): 2 × 20,000 ÷ 12,723 = 3.144.

Try it with toothpicks on lined paper. What did you get? 👇
