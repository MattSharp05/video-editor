# Instagram caption: s5 microwave myth

Your microwave does NOT cook food from the inside out 🔥❄️

Microwaves mostly heat the outer inch or so of thick food. The middle warms later, as that heat creeps inward.
That's why leftovers can be scalding at the edges and still cold in the middle. Let it stand a few minutes (or stir
when you can), and check it's hot all the way through.
