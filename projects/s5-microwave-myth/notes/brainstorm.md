# Picture brainstorm (fresh agent, 2026-11-19; direct.md brief, no production rules) — condensed

Draft lines: 1 "Most people think a microwave cooks food from the inside out." 2 "Watch what really happens." 3 "Microwaves
only reach about an inch into the food." 4 "They shake the water molecules in that outer layer, and that layer heats
up." 5 "The middle gets almost nothing. It only warms when heat creeps in from the outside, slowly." 6 "That's why a
frozen meal can be boiling at the edges and still icy in the middle." 7 "So stir it, and let it stand a minute: the heat
finishes the job."

Physics notes from the agent: show heat starting in a shell ~1 inch (1-4 cm) thick, never in the centre; molecules
flip back and forth with the reversing field (not "rubbing"); ice absorbs microwaves far less than liquid water, so a
thawed rim takes even more energy (runaway) while the frozen core stays cold; use a thick food (lasagne, frozen
burrito), since thin items are heated nearly through.

1. Myth: a cutaway lasagne with a glowing red core radiating outward, labelled as the myth, glitching like a broken
   hologram; egg yolk furnace + "NOPE" stamp; crayon lightning bolt into a potato's centre; a tiny chef inside a
   burrito revealed as a cardboard cutout; a sniper laser aimed at the centre (later all hits land on the outside).
2. Watch: X-ray mode, the door slides like a scanner, the food turns translucent; dive through the door mesh; the
   microwave winks; sports-replay telestrator; the dish sliced in half by a giant knife so we watch the cross-section.
3. An inch: wavy beams fade from bright to dim and stop at a dotted "~1 inch / 2.5 cm" line, middle dark; sunlight into
   a lake; rain on a sponge cake with a ruler; the food scaled to a planet (crust only); crayon ruler; tiny runners
   collapsing at a finish line one inch in.
4. Shake: water molecules flip back and forth ("2.45 billion times a second"), the band shifts blue to orange; a mosh
   pit at the rim vs a quiet coat-check middle; compass-needle molecules flipping; maracas; bouncy castle vs library.
5. Creep: the heat band bleeds inward in a timelapse, the clock slower than the timer; gossip chain; a stadium wave;
   bucket brigade to a shivering core; a snail with a flame; a city heatwave reaching a snowy downtown.
6. Icy middle: a fork pulls crunchy ice from the centre while the edges bubble; probe 95 C at the rim, -5 C in the
   centre; runaway thaw: waves bounce off the frozen core like tennis balls off a wall; toasted marshmallow; penguin on
   an ice rink in the middle with lava round the rim; crayon red ring and blue dot.
7. Stir and stand: a spoon swirls the red rim into the blue middle, even orange, a 1:00 timer and the last cold patch
   fades; shuffle cards / rest a steak; hot molecules walk warmth inward; ladle swirling a galaxy.

Giant devices: (1) heat-vision cutaway: the food sliced in half, thermal colours with a live scale; the myth version
glows at the centre (a glitching hologram), the real one lights a one-inch rim, bleeds inward, evens out after stirring.
(2) A molecule stadium crowd: only the outer rows hear the music. (3) A giant transparent ruler against the cut face,
the waves a tide that stops at the one-inch mark, then a slow seep.
