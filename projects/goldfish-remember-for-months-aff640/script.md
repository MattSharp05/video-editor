# Goldfish remember for months: script v1 (2026-11-27)

**Idea:** the three-second goldfish memory is a myth: goldfish learn a lever for food and even the hour it works,
and remember learned smells for at least three months. **Goal:** reach. Genre: myth-buster. Voice r3-m07, drafted at
2.7 words/s. Hook from the routine planner ("myth as question, then flat denial"). No avatar: no FACE lines.
Times are estimates until the VO is aligned.

Colour language: the goldfish (orange) is the lead; cyan = a cue the fish has learned (the lever's glow, the
feeding hour on the clock, the smell cloud); white pellets = food.

## Claims and sources (fresh research agent, 2026-11-27)
- Lever, one hour a day, pressing rose as feeding time approached: Gee, Stephenson & Wright 1994, J. Exp. Anal. Behav.
  62(1):1-13 (Plymouth, England), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1334363/ . Their test did not run for months:
  the film never says the lever fish remembered for months.
- Smells for food, still known at least 3 months later: Zippel et al. 1993, J. Comp. Physiol. A 172:81-90 (Göttingen,
  Germany), https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00214717 (abstract checked).
- Avoided: the Technion "sound, five months" study (species unnamed, almost surely not goldfish); "five months" or
  "years" for goldfish; rainbowfish 11 months.
