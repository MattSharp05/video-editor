# Instagram caption: s3-taipei-damper

There's a 660-tonne steel ball hanging near the top of Taipei 101. 🟡

It's a giant pendulum, tuned to the tower's own sway. When a typhoon pushes the top, the ball swings just behind it and tugs against the motion, while 8 hydraulic pistons underneath turn the swing into heat.
Taipei 101 says it cuts the sway by up to 40%. In Typhoon Soudelor (2015) it swung a full metre, its biggest move on record.

Would you go stand next to it during a typhoon? 👇
