# Instagram caption: s4 Sinkhole

That road you drive on every day? It could be hollow underneath. 🕳️

Rain picks up CO₂ and turns into a weak acid that slowly eats into limestone, opening cracks and holes.
Sticky clay can bridge the gap like an arch while it crumbles from below, so the empty space climbs toward the surface
and nothing shows on top.
Then the roof gets too thin, and it drops in seconds after growing for months or years.

Ever seen a sinkhole near you? 👇
