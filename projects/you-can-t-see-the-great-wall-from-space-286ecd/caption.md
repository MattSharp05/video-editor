Can you see the Great Wall of China from space? Most astronauts say no.

It's over 21,000 km long, but only about as wide as a road and built from the same earth and stone as the hills around it. From the Space Station, 400 km up, that's like spotting a pencil from 400 m away. What does shine from orbit: cities, airports and highways at night.
