# Instagram caption: s3-canal-locks

This ship climbs 9 meters, and not one pump lifts it. 🚢⬆️

The middle of the Panama Canal is Gatun Lake, 26 meters above the sea. Ships climb to it in three locks: concrete boxes that fill with lake water.
Close the gate, open a valve, and gravity does the rest. The water falls through tunnels in the walls and wells up through holes in the floor. In about 8 minutes the ship has floated up a whole step.
The catch: every crossing lets about 200 million liters of fresh water run out to sea, and only rain refills the lake.

Would you have guessed the canal runs on rain? 👇
