## Care rules for a murder story told to a general audience including kids
- Never show or describe the remains, how Cora died, the drug's effects, or the execution. "Human remains" is said
  once per part at most and is shown only as police lamps on a closed cellar floor or a covered sheet seen from a
  distance, never anything under it.
- Cora is the victim: show her warmly, as a lively singer and friend, never as a ghost, scare or joke.
- Ethel was found not guilty: never show her as a killer or accomplice in the murder.
- Real people are drawn as characters, not caricatures. Disputed details carry "Some say" / "Illustration" tags. The
  DNA twist is never presented as proof either way.
- Suspense comes from the chase and the clues, not fear: no jump scares, no blood, no weapons.
- The series must look clearly different from The Stolen Smile's paper toy theatre (its own palette, texture and fonts).
