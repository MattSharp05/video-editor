# Caught by Wireless: fact + tone check of scripts v0.1 (fresh agent, 2026-11-08; rewords applied in v1)

## Fact and tone check: "Caught by Wireless" (Crippen, 1910), parts 1–3

Nothing in the three scripts is false enough to sink it. I found **4 lines that are wrong as worded** and need a rewrite:
- P2-14, "Everyone knew, except the doctor"
- P2-16/17, "near Canada" and "A pilot climbed aboard"
- P3-4, "He walked up to Mr. Robinson"
- P3-17, "it all came down to … one message"

About ten more lines need small precision fixes. Tone is fine for kids throughout, with two small kindness tweaks for Ethel. I edited no files.

- The "race" is simplified: the two ships never sailed side by side.

**Part 3 caption caveats:**
- Dew's exact words differ between the trial record and his memoir; the line given is the shared opening.

- The courts have never reopened the case.

### Tone (all parts)
- **"Human remains":** gentle and truthful. Keep it, never show anything, and don't use "body parts", "mutilation" or "torso". Dew's own arrest words include "mutilation", so don't quote that line.
- **Death sentence:** "put to death, which was the law back then" is fine for kids.
- **Ethel:** only "accomplice"-type words are used ("helper"), and the series states her acquittal. Two small kindness fixes help: "was seen wearing" in P1-10, and you might drop the word "couple" in P2-8 (optional).
- **Cora:** she is presented warmly. Nothing is unkind to her.
- Nothing else is too scary.
