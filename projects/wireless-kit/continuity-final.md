# Caught by Wireless: continuity, accuracy and care judge on the finals (fresh agent, 2026-11-08)

**1. Continuity score: 8/10.** The three parts look like one series. The palette, halftone, fonts, captions, corner tab, plaque (settled by about 4 s in every part) and the telegram end card are the same throughout, and the cast matches the kit sheets. Crippen's look follows the record:
- Part 1: glasses and the heavy moustache, including the 8 July interview at 63–66 s.
- Part 2: the shave at 17–20 s, then a bare lip, no glasses and a felt hat for the rest of the part.
- Part 3: still bare-lipped at the arrest, in London (25 s) and in the dock (32–47 s).

The recaps use pictures the earlier parts really showed. Part 2 uses Vanished, The cellar and Ran away (matching R1–R3), dated 16 July. Part 3 opens on part 2's last picture, the pilot at the rail with the sun disc, then uses the race map and the pilot, dated 31 July. The Feather follows its arc: on the step, to Dew's bowler, in the pilot's pocket, in the bowler again, curled into a question mark, then up to the star. The care rules hold everywhere: closed cellar floor with lamps, Cora warm, Ethel in daylight, no execution picture, and the DNA result framed as "no match?" / "maybe wrong?". It loses points for a few dissolves and full-frame paper pages that are still there, near-empty frames, and tiny labels.
