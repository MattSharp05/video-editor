# Lactate run 01: research (fresh research agent, 2026-11-07)
Opened: Brooks 2020 JSHS (PMC7498672); Brooks 2022 J Physiol (PMC9188361); Goodwin/Gladden 2007 (PMC2769631); Cairns &
Lindinger 2025 EJAP (abstract); Cleveland Clinic "Lactic acid"; UC Davis Sports Medicine "What is lactate?"; Sonkodi 2022.
Snippets only: Pollak 2014, Robergs 2004 (disputed), Billat 2003, Houston Methodist (DOMS timing), Brooks 2018.
- Glycolysis makes lactate all the time; hard running speeds glycolysis and lactate rises far more than pyruvate [B].
  Not mainly lack of oxygen [A][B]: never say "no oxygen". "Glycolysis outpaces mitochondria" = textbook simplification.
- Lactate is fuel: heart, brain, other muscles burn it; liver makes glucose from it; 75-80% used in muscle [A][B].
- Body makes lactate, not lactic acid (<0.5% as acid at body pH) [C][D].
- Burn/slowdown: lactate alone does little harm; acidity plus phosphate cut force; acidosis not the sole cause [D];
  ache needs protons+lactate+ATP together [E]. Safe: "acid and other leftovers help slow you down".
- Threshold: lactate made faster than cleared; individual 1.4-7.5 mmol/L; training raises it via more clearance,
  mitochondria, MCT1 [A][B][C][J].
- DOMS: not lactate (clears in ~an hour; soreness 24-72 h; micro-damage) [H][K][F].
- Numbers: rest ~1-2 mmol/L; all-out 15-25 mmol/L [C][G].
