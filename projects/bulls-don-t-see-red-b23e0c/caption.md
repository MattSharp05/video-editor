# Caption (Instagram, @storytime_scroll)

Bulls don't hate red. They can't even see it as red. 🐂

Cattle have only two kinds of colour cells in their eyes (we have three), so a red cape looks dull and brownish to a bull. In a MythBusters test, a bull charged red, blue and white flags just the same, and chased the cowboys who moved while ignoring the man in red standing still. It's the movement, not the colour.
