## Draft caption
Bulls don't hate red. They can't even see it as red. Cattle have two kinds of colour cones (we have three), so the red cape
looks dull to them. In a test, a bull charged flags of any colour, and chased the people who moved: what draws the charge is the movement.

## Claims to verify
- Cattle are dichromats: two cone classes (S ~444-455 nm, M/L ~552-555 nm: range for cattle, goats and sheep); humans have three (Jacobs, Deegan & Neitz 1998,
  Visual Neuroscience 15:581).
- Dichromats with only S and M/L cones confuse red and green; long-wave red stimulates their M/L cone weakly, so a
  saturated red looks dull and darker (same; standard dichromat colour models, Brettel/Vienot 1997/1999).
- MythBusters "Red Rag to a Bull" (2007): the bull charged red, blue and white flags with no preference; with three
  people in the ring, it charged the one moving and left the still ones in red alone.

## Fact check (fresh agent, 2026-11-28)
- Fixed: "can't even see it" -> "can't even see it as red" (cattle detect red light; it just isn't a distinct hue).
  Card BULLS DON'T SEE RED.
- Fixed: "can't tell red from green" -> "look almost the same" (same hue, green lighter); picture: same yellow-brown
  hue, green lighter. Card RED ≈ GREEN.
- Fixed: "dull and dark" -> "dull and brownish" (cattle are close to a human deuteranope: red loses its colour, not
  much brightness).
- Fixed: "bulls charged blue capes and white capes" -> one bull, still flags (MythBusters 2007, episode 85).
- Fixed: the motion test used people: a man in a red suit stood still, two cowboys moved; the bull chased the cowboys.
- Holds: three vs two cone types (Jacobs, Deegan & Neitz 1998); "not the colour, the movement" (MythBusters,
  Britannica); "the red is for the crowd" (tradition and show; blood-hiding not claimed).
