Sunlight is every color. So why is the sky blue? 🌍

Air molecules bounce short blue light about 5x more than red (Rayleigh scattering), so blue reaches you from every part of the sky. At sunset the light crosses far more air, the blue is scattered away first, and only reds and oranges arrive.
