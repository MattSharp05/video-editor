# Instagram caption: s1 Shave thicker

Shaving doesn't make hair grow back thicker. 🪒

A razor only cuts the hair at the skin's surface. The root that grows it sits a few millimetres deeper and never feels
the blade.
Stubble feels coarser because shaving leaves a blunt end instead of the hair's natural tapered tip. Same width, just a
different end. Studies in 1928 and 1970 found shaved hair grew no faster, and in 1970, no thicker.

Were you told not to shave because it would grow back thicker?
